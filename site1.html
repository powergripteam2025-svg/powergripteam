ABOUT POWER GRIP TEAM

Born from a shared ambition to push student engineering to professional motorsport standards, Power Grip Team unites young Portuguese talent across mechanical design, aerodynamics, manufacturing, and brand management.

OUR STORY & COMPETITION

Competing in STEM Racing (F1 in Schools)

STEM Racing (formerly known globally as F1 in Schools) is the world's premier multidisciplinary STEM competition. Teams operate like real Formula 1 outfits—designing, analyzing, manufacturing, testing, and racing miniature compressed-air powered cars down a 20-meter track at speeds exceeding 70 km/h.

Beyond track speed, success requires mastering project management, enterprise portfolios, sponsorship acquisition, verbal presentations, and pit display engineering—mirroring the exact demands of international motorsport.
